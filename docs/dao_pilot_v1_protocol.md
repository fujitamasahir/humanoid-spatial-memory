# Proposed24-lineage DAO engineering pilot — NOT approved, NOT executed

This protocol is a concrete execution plan only. It does not authorize creating,
admitting or opening pilot/development/sealed payloads. Generator execution in
the current task is limited to explicitTEST engineering seeds. No DAO winner
is selected from this pilot; no efficacy claim is allowed.

## Proposed immutable allocation

24 pilot lineages, six per tree/loop/rooms/mixed family, two schedules each,
48 map-schedule conditions. Within each family lineage slots0..5 have dimensions
25x25,25x33,33x33,33x41,41x41,41x25. Pedestrian categories none/static/moving
repeat twice. Discovery long/occluded alternate; revisit frequent/rare uses
slot sequence frequent,frequent,rare,rare,frequent,rare. Persistent conditions
unchanged/add/remove/relocate/unchanged/relocate. This small pilot is a coverage
exercise, not balanced inference over every factor combination. Record category
counts, realized accepted corridor widths and rejection rates (certificate
selection can favor width3), openings/occluders/revisit descriptors, object
event counts, start/goal distances and schedule phase collisions.

Use independently custodian-derived pilot namespaces and master; dev80 (20 per
family) and sealed evaluation are separate, with no shared roots/seeds/streams.
Exact/near duplicates or unresolved admission => quarantine. No replacement
based on policy success. Max100 attempts per slot; failure stops for review.
Prior approved opaque signatures and full admission completion are prerequisites,
not inferred from currentTEST logs. Dense raster-graph matching scalability
must be resolved before approval; quarantine is safer than false uniqueness.

## Engineering acceptance (not efficacy selection)

Require complete schema/version/namespace coverage, certificate validity at
base and every persistent event, admitted unique roots, exact reproducibility,
all planned episodes/metrics present, cap and actual sensor-step equality,
selected-only shield counts, original G1/assets/data integrity, no forbidden
input accesses, finite output and hashes. Replay a fixed first condition/arm
for deterministic semantic parity only if prospectively authorized, logging its
extra episode separately. Any unwanted contact requires engineering review;
zero contacts in this small pilot is not a physical safety certificate.

Report failures/unreachable scenes, rescues/harms and all costs descriptively;
short failed movement does not count as efficiency. Audit unknown discovery,
stale-known verification, changed-known readings and route relevance separately.
Prediction errors need not be zero: report precision/recall undefined denominators,
counts/calibration limitations and conservative/optimistic error distributions.
Assess veto activation, Trigger/Combined equivalence and repeated fallback in
actual data without changing thresholds or ranking DAO winners. A failed
coverage mechanism triggers a future protocol review, not silent pilot tuning.

Owner's original target is improved completion OR preserved completion with
fewer paid scans, zero contacts.3-point completion NI and10% joint sensing/
movement benefit are scientific PROPOSALS; they cannot be retroactively imposed
as the original target or claimed approved. Pilot does not establish either.

## Sample-size procedure after engineering approval

Use completion discordance and schedule correlation to estimate paired lineage
variance on approved development data, with conservative uncertainty bounds.
Simulate the exact fixed-sequence analysis under completion loss0,1point and
3point null boundary; sample sizes80/200/800 here are sensitivity settings,
not population generation. Check CI coverage/type-I error and joint conditional
cost/contact behavior. Normal/percentile approximations can under-cover; rare
or degenerate outcomes require conservative bounds or a revised prespecified
analysis method. Do not choose a margin because200 is affordable.

If NI3points is scientifically required, plan enough independent lineages for
power under plausible differences/variance and multiplicity; paired SD.5 at
true difference0 implies about2181 before joint endpoint constraints. An
owner-approved affordable study may instead report estimation intervals and
engineering feasibility without a NI/superiority declaration. Sequential or
two-stage sampling is possible only with predeclared stopping/error control
and custodian review; no optional stopping on favorable sealed outcomes.
Keep sealed n undecided until approved variance/coverage/power checks support it.
