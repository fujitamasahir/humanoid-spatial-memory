# Pre-scan public omission features: frozen retrospective diagnostics

Base 1813cb3c94ff79e12500c01f99991ecf9a539310. Preserve the completed
single-omission experiment: 60 tests,52 untreated replays,149 interventions
with matching untreated prefixes. 49 independent one-at-a-time interventions
saved paid scans without worsening paired common-success movement or contacts;
81 left net paid scans unchanged and19 increased paid scans. All6 goal/contact
harm cases had at least one changed-known cell in the omitted reading,
but17 of49 no-worse savings also had a changed-known cell. This association
does not establish a causal role of changed-known cells, nor can their
unobserved realized contents be used to decide before scanning.

## Fixed diagnostic masks

Evaluate all masks, including unfiltered and simple controls, rather than
select a winner after seeing results. A known prefix requires an available
route with all prefix cells observed free. Stable prefix additionally
requires no recorded recent change within its band and no predicted visible
stale occupied cell within its band. This term is shorthand for lack of
these particular risk indicators, not evidence of physical stability.
Other masks add a known visible band, protection of the first noninitial
scan (at least two prior paid readings), unchanged previous reading, high
predicted view overlap, or unchanged pose and heading; a fully known-route
mask checks the full nominal route. These masks are candidate diagnostic
conditions, NOT deployable scheduling rules.

Before joining outcomes, reconstruct and save all149 rows, asserting
prior paid count and original DAO frontier/route feature agreement.
The report then joins the separately frozen intervention ledger by
scene index, safety condition and step. Count no-worse common-success
savings captured, goal/contact harms, common-success movement harms,
paid/combined-sensing increases, and distinct scenes per mask. Separate
safety versions and original/prospective scene roles; also record
conflicting observed labels for identical chosen aggregate signatures.
A zero-harm mask on these reused, correlated cases is not a safety
guarantee, generalization result or evidence that simultaneous skipping
works. Do not fit a predictor, set a policy threshold, select an online
policy or execute another navigation matrix in this task.
